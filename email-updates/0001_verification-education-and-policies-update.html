✓ Get verified

Verification adds a verified tick to your name so students know you're a genuine, reviewed tutor. Profiles people can trust get more enquiries — and verified tutors are ranked above unverified ones in search and browse results.

How it works

1

Request it. Open Settings (or the final step of onboarding) and click Request verification. Tip: fill out your profile first — subjects, rate, bio and education — so there's plenty for us to review.

2

We review. Our team checks your profile, usually within a few days. You can keep editing in the meantime — the status shows as pending in your settings.

3

You're verified. Once approved, the tick appears on your public profile and your browse card straight away, and we'll email you to confirm.

Verification is free, and you only need to request it once.

🎓 High school & university

Education entries now distinguish between high school and university. When you add a school in Settings, pick its level from the new dropdown. Your public profile labels each entry, and your tutor card highlights your high school (or your university if no high school is listed) — so students instantly see your background.

Your existing schools have been set to high school by default — take a moment to update any that should be university.
